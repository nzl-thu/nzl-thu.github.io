I am a Ph.D. student in **Control Science and Engineering** in the Department of Automation at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), working with [Prof. Gao Huang](http://www.gaohuang.net/) at [LeapLab](https://www.leaplab.ai/). I received my B.Eng. in Automation from Tsinghua University in 2022, ranking **2nd out of 175** students.

My research focuses on **parallel sampling strategies for generative AI**, spanning **image generation** and **language inference**. I study how sampling policies, model architectures, and reinforcement learning can improve discrete generative models and diffusion language models.

I am the sole first author of [**The Flexibility Trap**](https://nzl-thu.github.io/the-flexibility-trap), which received the [**ICML 2026 Outstanding Paper Award**](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/) (**2/23,918 submissions**) and was selected for an **Oral** presentation. Our work **InsightTok** also received the **LoViF @ ECCV 2026 Workshop Outstanding Paper Award** and an **Oral** presentation.

I am currently a **research intern at DeepSeek** (Aug. 2026 - present), working on model architecture design in the **Pretraining (Backbone) team**. Previously, I was a research intern at **Alibaba's Future Living Lab** (now part of the ATH business group, Jun. 2024 - Aug. 2026), where I led university-industry research on **The Flexibility Trap** and **AdaGen**. At [BAAI](https://www.baai.ac.cn/english.html) (Oct. 2022 - Jun. 2023), I led [MUSE-Pytorch](https://github.com/baaivision/MUSE-Pytorch), an open-source reproduction of Google's Muse later listed as a reference implementation by ByteDance's [1d-tokenizer (TiTok / RAR / MaskGen)](https://github.com/bytedance/1d-tokenizer).

I lead an **NSFC research project for doctoral students** (2026-2027) and participate in the **CAST Young Science and Technology Talent Cultivation Program, Doctoral Student Track** (2025-2027). I received the **National Scholarship for Doctoral Students** in both **2025 and 2026**. See my [CV]({{ '/cv/' | relative_url }}) for education, experience, grants, and honors, and my [publications]({{ '/publications/' | relative_url }}) for the full list of research works.
