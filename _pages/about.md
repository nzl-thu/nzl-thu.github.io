I am a Ph.D. student at [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), advised by [Prof. Gao Huang](http://www.gaohuang.net/) at [LeapLab](https://www.leaplab.ai/). I am also a research intern at DeepSeek, working on model architectures.

I study **parallel sampling for generative AI**, particularly for image generation and diffusion language models. My work [The Flexibility Trap](https://nzl-thu.github.io/the-flexibility-trap) received the [ICML 2026 Outstanding Paper Award](https://blog.icml.cc/2026/07/05/announcing-the-icml-2026-awards/).
