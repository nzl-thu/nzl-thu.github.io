I am a Ph.D. student at Tsinghua University, advised by Prof. Gao Huang, and a research intern on the pretraining team at DeepSeek.

My research focuses on large language models, especially model architecture and efficient decoding, including diffusion language models. My work The Flexibility Trap received the ICML 2026 Outstanding Paper Award. Earlier, I worked on parallel decoding for image generation.
